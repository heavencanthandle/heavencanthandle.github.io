Timber Framing

Heavy timber porticos, trusses, and pavilions cut and raised by hand, built to carry snow and look good doing it.

What We Build

  • Entry porticos and gable trusses over garage and front doors
  • King-post and queen-post trusses for great rooms and porches
  • Exposed rafter tails, outlookers, and brackets
  • Covered patios, pavilions, and carports
  • Timber posts and beams on existing decks and porches
  • Traditional joinery with pegs or hidden steel, your call

How It Works

  • Design sized for your snow and wind loads, with sketches so you can see the proportions before anything is cut
  • Material sourced as rough-sawn Douglas fir or cedar, planed or left textured
  • Cut and raise on site, finished with oil or stain to match your siding and trim
